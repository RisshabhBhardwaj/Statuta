| Provision | Disposition | Phase 0 record |

| § 10(a) definitions | Context | Defined terms require a future definition model; no standalone runtime rule. |

| § 10(e) confidentiality/access | Coverage gap | FOIA and ownership/access facts are outside a query decision. |
| § 10(f) policy | Obligation gap | Policy content/fulfilment is not tracked in v0.1. |

| § 10(i)–(k) enforcement | Out of scope | Remedies, agency action, and discipline are not evaluated in v0.1. |

No provision is silently omitted. The only candidate is intentionally non-released until every linked exception is encoded or accepted as a documented gap in review.
